# EraClash Basketball — Wave 2 guide (first-time testers)

Thanks for trying EraClash Basketball. This is a private beta. You are one of a
handful of people seeing it, and you are seeing it **without a tutorial on
purpose**: how you find your way in is the thing being tested.

## Getting in

1. Open the private beta address from your invite.
2. Enter your personal access key on the "Private preview" screen. You stay
   signed in for a week on that device.
3. Keep the address and key to yourself — please don't forward, post or
   screenshot them.

## What to do (about 25–40 minutes)

**1. Start playing.** Begin the game that looks like the main EraClash
experience. That's the whole instruction. Go with your instinct.

**2. Finish one game.** Play it through to the final score.

**3. Look around the result.** Explore whatever interests you in the result —
there is no right order and nothing you can break.

**4. Do whatever you feel like next.** Play again, try something else, or stop.
Any of those is a useful answer.

**5. If you can, do steps 1–2 once on your phone as well.**

## Telling us what you thought

After a game, and under **Help & Settings → Wave 2 feedback**, there is a short
panel: pick the step you just did, rate a few statements from 1 to 5, choose an
issue if something went wrong, and add a comment if you like. One submission per
step is enough; you can resubmit and it replaces the earlier one.

Please don't put names, emails or anything personal in comments.

## Ground rules

- There are no wrong answers. Confusion is data, not failure.
- Nothing here is real money, real accounts or real leagues.
- If the beta breaks, tell the person who invited you.
