# EraClash Basketball — Wave 2 guide (returning testers)

You tested EraClash Basketball in Wave 1. Wave 2 changes the **entrance**, the
**look**, and how you **place players** in Dream Matchup. The engine that
decides games is Candidate 4 — a newer calibration than Wave 1's Candidate 3 —
but this wave is about the experience around it.

You have a **new address and a new key** for Wave 2. Your Wave 1 key does not
open it, and your Wave 1 feedback is kept exactly as you left it.

## Part 1 — The entrance (do this before reading Part 3)

1. Open the Wave 2 address and sign in with your Wave 2 key.
2. Start a Chaos Clash and roll once.
3. Tap the EraClash logo at the top, then find your way back to the game you
   started. Did you get the same board back?
4. Only now, tell us in the feedback panel: was starting easier or harder than
   Wave 1, and why?

## Part 2 — Dream Matchup placement

1. Open Dream Matchup and add **Kevin Durant** (or any player listed at more
   than one position) to Team Gold.
2. Notice what the board offers you, then place him.
3. Add a player who plays only one position — does he land where you expect?
4. Add a player whose only fit is a slot that is already taken. Try the swap.
5. Use **Undo**.

Rate "placing a player in a position was clear" in the feedback panel
(task R2) and describe anything that surprised you.

## Part 3 — The look

Compare the new look with what you remember from Wave 1, across the lobby, the
Time Arena, the result panel, the full report, and your phone. We are not going
to tell you what we hoped you'd say. Rate comfort, premium feel, how "EraClash"
it feels, and result clarity (task R3).

## Feedback panel

After a game, and under **Help & Settings → Wave 2 feedback**. One submission
per task is enough; a resubmission replaces the earlier one. No personal
information in comments, please.
