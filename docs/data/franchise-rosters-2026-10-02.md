# Franchise roster proposals — 2026-10-02

B3 content data only. Basketball is the sole project affected. No player card, possession algorithm, rating, calibration parameter, or protected candidate is changed.

## Selection standard and source limits

Every five is a curated proposal using the existing 381 player-decade cards, in PG / SG / SF / PF / C order. Existing `positions` eligibility is authoritative, and each lineup has five different people. The selection combines historical importance, a credible connection to the franchise, position coverage, and available cards. It is not an official ranking, a complete historical roster, a greatest-five claim established by the cited sources, or a forecast of the scheduled contemporary teams.

The sources below establish historical context and franchise connections for the notes; they do not validate every existing card's statistics or endorse these five-player selections. Each source URL was read or returned by an official league/team source during this task. Existing card team labels supply ordinary affiliation evidence; two explicit exceptions are Alonzo Mourning's Charlotte tenure and Chris Paul's old Hornets label, which belongs to New Orleans in his 2000s card. The official Timberwolves number history corroborates Cassell and Butler. Other questionable membership substitutions are avoided.

The card data is intentionally reused without reslicing statistics. A decade card may include multiple clubs or use an inherited unverified statistical basis. The existing player-card-stat-basis documentation reports 310 LEGACY_UNVERIFIED entries; this task does not convert them to verified franchise-specific season data. See `docs/simulation-v3/player-card-stat-basis.md` and `docs/simulation-v3/translation-doctrine.md`. These are franchise-themed era-card lineups, not five newly researched franchise-prime statistical records.

Automatic watch mode uses the existing neutral staff (`neutral`) for both teams, 2020s era rules, and neutral court. Scheduled home/away order is descriptive and adds no court advantage. Take Control permits the user's supported coach decision through the normal game path. A selection is legal because the source card permits the slot, not because the player historically spent an entire career in that one role.

## Lineage and naming decisions

- Atlanta includes St. Louis; Brooklyn includes ABA New York and New Jersey; the Clippers include Buffalo Braves; Philadelphia includes Syracuse; Sacramento includes the Royals; Washington includes its Bullets eras. Jazz history is distinct from the later New Orleans franchise.
- Charlotte includes the original Charlotte and Bobcats lineage. Chris Paul's New Orleans Hornets card is not inserted into Charlotte. Oklahoma City is deliberately scoped to 2008 onward; Seattle legends are not relabeled Oklahoma City.
- `poolIds` is a conservative adapter of the existing card team labels plus the curated five. It is **not** a researched player-by-player career-affiliation database. A player omitted from `poolIds` is not evidence that he never played for that franchise. Historical drafting filters should disclose that data limit rather than asserting complete franchise rosters.
- `franchiseDisplayName(f,{neutralNaming:true})` uses city + All-time. The two Los Angeles entries are Los Angeles Gold and Los Angeles Blue so they remain distinct. This is exposure reduction, not legal clearance. The central `NEUTRAL_TEAM_NAMING` flag defaults OFF; display helpers accept that central resolved value. No logos, player photography, or third-party marks are created by these scripts.

## Availability compromises requiring review

Brooklyn lacks Brook Lopez, so Williams uses existing C eligibility and Durant PF eligibility. Memphis lacks Tony Allen, Rudy Gay, and a Memphis-era Randolph card; its legal Conley/Morant/Bane perimeter avoids calling an unproven rookie an all-time icon. New Orleans lacks matching-era Holiday/West cards; Murray at SG is a particularly debatable available-card choice. Portland lacks 1970s Walton, so its Celtics-era Walton card is not misassigned; Mychal Thompson is the available center. Toronto Leonard and Minnesota Butler have short franchise tenures. Contemporary Orlando forwards are not established career-long franchise selections. These choices should remain visibly curated proposals until the owner reviews them.
